\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Freezing.} The protocol, split manifests, descriptions and the list of primary contrasts were committed before the new
evaluations (git tag \texttt{protocol-frozen-v1}); Ottavo-R was added and frozen before it was evaluated. All historical test sets
had already been observed in an earlier exploratory study by the same team, whose results are kept as a separate, unverified layer.

\paragraph{Label budgets.} Budgets count every task label used: $k \in \{5, 20\}$ examples per class, or the full training set. For
each seed in $\{17, 42, 123\}$ the per-class subsets are nested and identical across methods; the seed also controls training
randomness, so seed variability combines both sources. Decision models with label names (N) use no labelled example of the task.
With descriptions (D) this holds on Ottavo, but on Banking77 and MASSIVE-it the descriptions were written from labelled training
examples (Section~\ref{sec:data}). An external review pointed out that this conflicts with counting every label used; we therefore
added, after the main analysis, a post-hoc family P2N that compares the best decision model with label names against each compact
classifier, and we use it as the zero-label comparison. The pre-declared family P2 (with D) is still reported.

\paragraph{Recipe selection without test data.} For $k$-shot budgets, the learning rate and number of epochs of mmBERT and the LoRA/QLoRA
decoders are selected by 3-fold stratified cross-validation inside the budget on seed 17 and then frozen for the other seeds. A constant
learning rate after a 10-step warm-up makes evaluations at different epochs of one run equivalent to runs of different length. In the
full regime the epoch is selected on a 10\% stratified split of the training set. TF-IDF selects $C$ by the same cross-validation.
SetFit uses the default recipe of \citet{tunstall2022setfit} without tuning (contrastive fine-tuning capped at 2{,}000 steps in the full
regime). The \emph{original recipe} of the exploratory study (one-cycle schedule, $\lceil 4000/n \rceil$ epochs clamped to $[3,40]$)
is re-run unchanged as a reference.

\paragraph{API protocol.} Every call is cached by provider, model, request body and repetition index, so identical requests are never
re-billed and repeated requests are explicit. Refusals and failed calls remain in the denominator; we report coverage separately. The
distribution over options returned by the API, renormalised over the valid options, is the probability used for calibration; the API
\texttt{confidence} field is stored but not used. Expensive conditions (examples in the prompt, option-order permutations, repetitions)
use a frozen stratified subset of 300 test messages (288 for Ottavo).

\paragraph{Metrics and statistics.} The primary metric is macro-F1 over classes present in the gold labels. Differences between systems
are paired differences on identical examples with 95\% percentile intervals from 2{,}000 bootstrap replicates of the test examples
\citep{efron1993bootstrap}; for supervised systems the statistic is the mean over seeds. Holm's correction \citep{holm1979} is applied
within each family of contrasts; confidence intervals are not adjusted for multiplicity. With 2{,}000 replicates the resolution of
$p$ is $0.0005$, and $p$-values below $0.001$ are reported as such. We do not claim equivalence for non-significant differences: a
difference whose interval includes zero is \emph{not resolved}, not a tie. Calibration uses ECE with 15
equal-width bins on the maximum class probability and the multiclass Brier score.
